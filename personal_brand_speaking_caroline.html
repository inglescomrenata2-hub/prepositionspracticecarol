Debate — 90 segundos por posicao

Afirmacao 1 90s

“In corporate marketing, personal brand matters more than technical performance.”

Voce concorda? Discorda? Tem uma posicao mais matizada? Defenda.

Para concordar

  • Visibility shapes career trajectory
  • Trust opens doors before results can
  • Narrative frames how performance is read

Para discordar

  • Results are the foundation of credibility
  • Personal brand without delivery = empty image
  • In data-driven teams, numbers speak louder
